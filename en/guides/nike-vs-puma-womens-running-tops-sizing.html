Nike vs PUMA: running tops.

We compare both brands' women's tops guides and use one current long-sleeve garment from each as an example, without converting their sizes.

Updated 26 Oct 20268 min readWomen · Running
Our advice

Start with your measurements and the size guide for the brand you plan to buy. Then check how the exact product describes its fit: Nike Swift is described as loose and PUMA VELOCITY as slim. These are specific garment examples, not a rule for every top or a size conversion.

Pages and colour variants can change. The header is an editorial illustration inspired by long-sleeve running tops, not an official photograph or an exact reproduction of the linked products. Recheck the guide and item you intend to buy.

What this comparison covers

We focus on women's long-sleeve running tops and each brand's general women's tops guide. We do not claim that one brand always runs larger or smaller across every category, and we do not carry one product's advice over to another.

Nike's chart uses body measurements. PUMA's general page presents international size conversions and separate instructions for measuring the body. Since the formats differ, a letter is not an automatic conversion between brands.

Nike: start with your measurements and check the cut

Nike's official women's tops guide lists bust, waist and hip ranges. Measure according to Nike's diagram and compare your figures with the right category. The chart describes the body; it does not necessarily give the finished garment measurements.

As one example, the Nike Swift long-sleeve top (style IM7542-010) is described as loose. The page checked shows its model wearing S. That helps explain how Nike presents that particular top, but it cannot predict the fit on another person or be compared directly with PUMA's S.

If you prefer less room, consider the fit you want and look for a product-specific recommendation. Do not choose a different size just because both items are labelled for running; check the shoulders, chest, sleeves and length of the exact top.

PUMA: distinguish size conversion from body measurements

PUMA's official size guide provides an international clothing conversion table and separate instructions for measuring bust, waist and hips. Follow the current chart for the right category; a conversion between letters or countries does not describe how a particular top fits.

The PUMA VELOCITY Long Sleeve Running Top Women (style 526581_01) is our product example. The page checked describes a slim fit and shows its model wearing S. That detail belongs to the item on that page; it does not replace body measurements or predict your fit.

For both brands, the correct chart depends on the category and a product listing may change by season or colour. Reopen the pages before buying and follow any specific sizing advice shown beside the product's size selector.

Compare your body with a chart and one garment with another

Use your body measurements to choose within the relevant size guide. If you own a running top with a fit you like, lay it flat without stretching and measure across the chest, shoulders, sleeve and length. Check that the new listing measures from the same points; if it does not publish garment measurements, do not invent an equivalence from the photo.

Think about how you will wear the top. A loose garment and a slim one may feel different even when both charts point to the same letter. Try it with your usual base layer and move your arms forward and overhead. Check that the sleeves do not pull and the hem does not ride up uncomfortably.

If you are between sizes

First check that you followed the brand's measuring diagram. Then decide what fit you want and which area matters most: chest, shoulders, sleeves or length. The next size may add room but also change volume and length; the right choice depends on the pattern and product listing.

The same approach applies to other sportswear. Compare Sprinter and Decathlon running tights or read our Nike and adidas sweatshirt guide. In each case, use each brand's own chart and avoid converting a size between products.

Before you choose

  • Open the women's tops chart for the brand you are buying.
  • Measure bust, waist and hips according to its instructions.
  • Read the fit of the exact top, not just the category.
  • Use model size and height as context only.
  • Compare a reference top using the same measurement points.
  • Record brand, style, tried size and how it felt in motion.

The practical conclusion is not that a Nike S equals a PUMA S. Use each chart on its own terms, understand the cut of the garment you want and compare measurements taken in the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Do Nike and PUMA running tops fit the same?

There is no universal conversion. Nike shows body ranges for women's tops; PUMA's general guide shows size conversions with separate instructions for measuring. Check the right category and product.

Which tops are used as examples?

The Nike Swift women's long-sleeve running top, style IM7542, and the PUMA VELOCITY Long Sleeve Running Top Women, style 526581. They are listing examples, not equivalents.

Can I compare the S sizes shown on both models?

No. The product pages show different models and tops with different fits. Use your measurements with each official guide and read the product recommendation.

Does this guide cover men's tops or other garments?

Not directly. It focuses on women's running tops. Find the size chart for the category you intend to buy.
