Skechers vs New Balance: walking shoe sizing.

We compare both brands’ official sizing information and use one walking model from each to show what to check on its product page.

Updated 2 Nov 20268 min readWomen · Walking
Our advice

Measure both feet and check the guide for the brand you are buying. Then open the exact product page and review length, width and fit. A Skechers size does not automatically convert to the same New Balance size.

This is a brand-to-brand comparison. Cassy and Fresh Foam X Walking 880 v7 are specific examples for reading the product pages; we do not present them as equivalent models or declare a winner.

The product examples in this guide are women’s styles so the sizing category stays consistent. Collections, sizes and available widths can change, so check the links again before buying.

What this guide compares

We focus on walking shoes and the options shown on the official pages checked. We are not comparing cushioning, support or performance as medical advice; the aim is to explain how to choose a size and review the fit of a specific product.

A chart may present foot measurements, size conversions and product widths in different ways. Read the headings and category before choosing. The same EU number can appear on both pages, but that does not prove that both lasts have the same room.

Skechers: check the exact model options

The GO WALK Arch Fit 2.0 - Cassy page shows a size guide beside the product selector and medium and wide width options. The page advises choosing medium width if you do not usually buy narrow or wide shoes. Check the variant and availability on the exact page you plan to order.

For this example, Skechers describes a technical mesh upper and fixed elastic laces. That affects how you assess the fit: the laces cannot be adjusted like traditional laces. Check space and hold in this exact shoe, without assuming that all GO WALK styles fit the same way.

New Balance: EU length and width are separate details

The official New Balance size and width guide for women links EU sizes to foot length in centimetres and publishes measurements for different widths. Use the columns for your category and treat the foot length as that brand’s guide, not a universal conversion to Skechers.

The Fresh Foam X Walking 880 v7 page lists EU sizes 35–44, Standard and Wide widths, and traditional adjustable laces. The page describes its fit as true to size for most people; treat that as information for this model, not a guarantee of your personal fit.

Measure your feet before looking at the label

Place a sheet of paper against a wall, set your heel against it and mark your longest toe. Measure both feet and keep the larger measurement. Repeat in the socks you plan to walk in and follow the brand’s own method if it provides a printable guide or diagram.

Use that length in the relevant official chart. If you also know the width of your forefoot, check whether the brand and model offer more than one width. Do not compare a “39” directly with another “39”: each chart and each model needs to be read on its own.

Check the fit while walking

When trying the shoes, fasten them as you normally would and walk for a few minutes on a safe surface. Check that your toes do not hit the front as you step, your heel does not slip uncomfortably and you do not feel a pressure point along the sides. Judge the fit on your foot, not just by the number on the box.

If the length feels right but the shoe is tight across the width, first check whether that model comes in another width. Sizing up can add length without solving the cause of the pressure. If the model does not offer an option that works for you, look for another cut or shape within the brand.

If you are between sizes or widths

Check your foot measurement and selected category again. Then review the recommendation on the exact product page and decide whether the issue is length, width or hold. For New Balance, check the Standard and Wide options for that product; for Skechers, reopen the width selector for the specific variant.

Do not choose from a general conversion or the size of another model. Save the brand, model, size, width and how each area felt while walking. You can also read what to do when you are between two sizes and how to check autumn footwear fit.

Before you choose

  • Measure both feet and use the larger one as your reference.
  • Open the correct category guide for each brand.
  • Check length and width separately.
  • Read the width options and closure details for the exact model.
  • Try walking and check toes, heel and sides.
  • Save the brand, model, size and width together as one reference.

The most useful way to compare Skechers and New Balance is to apply each guide separately and try the exact model. That keeps two matching labels from being mistaken for the same fit.

Frequently asked questions

Do Skechers and New Balance walking shoes fit the same?

There is no universal conversion. Check each brand’s guide and the exact product page; a matching EU size alone does not describe the same width or fit.

Which models are used as examples?

Skechers GO WALK Arch Fit 2.0 - Cassy and New Balance Fresh Foam X Walking 880 v7. They show what to look for on each product page; they are not equivalent models.

What if the length feels right but the shoe is tight across the width?

Check whether the product comes in another width. Do not automatically size up, as that may add length without solving the pressure along the sides.

Does this guide cover men’s shoes?

The product examples and sizing details on this page refer to women’s models. For another category, use that category’s official guide and product page.
