Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026.

sizes.es uses essential local storage to remember your language and analytics choice. Google Analytics 4 is activated only when you expressly accept analytics cookies.

Necessary storage

sizes-preferred-locale remembers your chosen language. sizes-analytics-consent-v1 remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics. Both are stored in your browser and are not sent to Google.

Optional analytics cookies

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 may create _ga and _ga_<identifier> to distinguish visits and maintain session state. We configure them with a maximum duration of 13 months. User and event data are retained in Analytics for 14 months; Google signals and advertising personalisation remain disabled.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choice

Accept and reject are equally accessible. You can change your decision through the “Cookie settings” button available on the site. When you withdraw consent, we delete the Google Analytics cookies accessible from this site and stop loading the measurement tag.
