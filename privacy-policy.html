Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026.

The Sizes Android app stores user-entered profiles, measurements, brands, sizes, notes and photos on the device. It requires no account and includes no advertising, Firebase or analytics SDK.

Local data and backups

App data is not sent to our servers and Android automatic backup is disabled. Backup files exported voluntarily by the user contain readable, unencrypted data and should be handled carefully.

Network connections

Core features work offline. Opening “Tips” may request the catalogue, images or an article from sizes.es. These requests do not include profiles, measurements, sizes, notes or photos. The server may process standard technical data such as IP address, time, user agent and security logs.

Analytics on this website

The public pages on sizes.es use Google Analytics 4 only if you accept analytics cookies. Before acceptance, the Google tag is not downloaded and no measurement data is sent. If you accept, we measure aggregated page views, language, page type and actions such as opening Google Play, switching language or sharing an article. Advertising and ad personalisation remain disabled.

You can reject or withdraw consent at any time through “Cookie settings”. See the Cookie Policy for cookie names, duration and provider information.

Photos and sharing

Label photos are processed and stored locally. “Share” creates a summary image without body measurements or label photos and opens the device share sheet. Sizes does not receive that image.

Third parties and deletion

Sizes does not sell data and the app uses no advertising or analytics SDK. If you consent to website measurement, Google acts as our analytics provider. If you open an article or share with another app, that service's policy also applies. You can delete local information in the app, clear its data or uninstall it.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@sizes.es.
